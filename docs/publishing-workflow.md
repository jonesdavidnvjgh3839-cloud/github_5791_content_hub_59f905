# Publishing Workflow

All content published to the Helios Analytics Content Hub follows a review-then-publish process:

1. **Draft** — Authors create and iterate on content in a draft state.
2. **Review** — Content is submitted for editorial and technical review.
3. **Approve** — Reviewers approve the content once all feedback has been addressed.
4. **Publish** — Approved content is merged and published to the hub.

Only content that has passed review may be published to the hub.
